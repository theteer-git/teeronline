Teer Formula

A Teer formula is a method used to organize or narrow possible numbers before a result. TeerOnline separates established Teer concepts from patterns discovered later in its own historical research.

  2. Teer Formula

House and Ending

For a two-digit result such as 47, House is the first digit (4) and Ending is the second digit (7). House 4 represents 40–49; Ending 7 represents 07, 17, 27, 37, 47, 57, 67, 77, 87 and 97. One House plus one Ending identifies one full number.

Combining multiple Houses and Endings

If Houses are 1, 4 and 5 while Endings are 0 and 6, the Cartesian combinations are 10, 16, 40, 46, 50 and 56. Three Houses × two Endings produces six candidates.

Why candidate count matters

A method that outputs three numbers and a method that outputs sixty numbers do not provide the same narrowing even if both happen to contain the result. Candidate-set size must therefore be measured with hit rate.

TeerOnline prediction limit

A tested method may output 1–6 candidates. If its natural set contains 7 or more, the output is NO PREDICTION. Candidates must not be removed after calculation simply to force the set below the limit.

Paired values

The paired-value mapping used in Teer analysis is 0↔5, 1↔6, 2↔7, 3↔8 and 4↔9. The mapping can be applied to House or Ending. Claims about what a paired value will do next remain hypotheses until historical testing supports them.

Blocked House and Ending

A House or Ending is blocked for a selected historical period when it does not appear in that sample. If blocked Houses are 1, 4, 5, 8 and blocked Endings are 0, 6, 7, their combinations are 10, 16, 17, 40, 46, 47, 50, 56, 57, 80, 86 and 87. Construction of the set is factual; what happens in the following week must be tested.

Continuous-five Ending observation

The non-wrapping five-digit Ending blocks are 0–4, 1–5, 2–6, 3–7, 4–8 and 5–9. The condition does not require all five digits to appear; one appearance is enough for the whole block not to remain absent. FR and SR must be tested independently.

Forecast (FC)

Forecast links an FR selection with an SR selection. If the forecast is FR 42 and SR 76, both round selections must match for the complete FC to match. An FR-only or SR-only match is not a successful complete FC.

Traditional methods vs TeerOnline research

House, Ending, paired values, Forecast and other documented Teer practices should be labelled as existing methods. Weekly absence relationships, cross-session transitions, recurrence patterns and other rules discovered from TeerOnline data should be labelled as TeerOnline historical research, not retroactively described as traditional formulas.

How a formula is tested

Define the condition, candidate construction and target before testing. Find every qualifying historical case, record the candidates and candidate count, then compare with the actual result. A pattern with two or more historical failures is rejected under TeerOnline’s strict research rule; a minimum sample threshold is also required.

Discover first, test later

Older data may be used to discover a candidate pattern. Later unseen data should test it. Live use should be limited to patterns that already passed validation. Future results must never leak into an earlier historical prediction.

No prediction is a valid result

The system may return NO VALID PATTERN, NO PREDICTION — CANDIDATE SET TOO LARGE, or NO PREDICTION — INSUFFICIENT HISTORICAL EVIDENCE. Abstention is preferable to weakening a method until it produces numbers every day.

Formula output is not a declared result

Formula candidates are analysis produced before a round. A declared FR or SR is the confirmed result after the round. After declaration, the system may score the earlier candidate set, but it must not rewrite that set to fit the known result.
