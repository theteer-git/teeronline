About Shillong Night Teer

This page contains Shillong Night, also called Night 1. The First Round is at 8:45 PM IST and the Second Round at 9:45 PM IST, so both rounds fall in the same evening, before midnight. It is separate from Shillong Teer, Shillong Morning and Shillong Night 2, even though all four use the Shillong name.

Shillong Night does not take Sunday off. A week in this list can therefore include seven playing evenings.

Choosing Sunday in the Day of Week filter lists Sunday evenings only. Combine it with the round filter to see just the FR (8:45 PM) or just the SR (9:45 PM).

Shillong Night Teer is played in Shillong, the same city as the day game. First Round (FR) is declared at 8:45 PM and Second Round (SR) at 9:45 PM IST.

Shillong Night Teer at a glance

  • Game: Shillong Night, also called Night 1, the evening game
  • First Round (FR): 8:45 PM IST
  • Second Round (SR): 9:45 PM IST
  • Playing days: every day of the week, with no Sunday off
  • Filters: date, number and round

Searching a Shillong Night result

Select Filter, then choose Same date, every year to check a known evening across every year on record, or Find a number to see the dates on which it was declared, with round as optional narrowing. Newest evenings come first, and the table shows 50 rows by default, with 25 and 100 available for a shorter or longer stretch.

House and Ending in Shillong Night results

In a result such as 58 the House is 5 and the Ending is 8. Night results are two-digit numbers like any other, and FR and SR are each read on their own. The Teer Formula guide has more on House, Ending and paired values.

Shillong Night and Shillong Night 2

Shillong Night 2 starts later, at 11:10 PM, and its SR is at 12:10 AM. It has its own page: Shillong Night 2 previous results. For the daytime Shillong games, see Shillong Teer and Shillong Morning.

Shillong Night Teer results: common questions

What time are the Shillong Night results?

FR is scheduled for 8:45 PM IST and SR for 9:45 PM IST, so both rounds fall before midnight.

Is Shillong Night the same as Shillong Night 2?

No. Night 2 is a later game with FR at 11:10 PM and SR at 12:10 AM IST. It has its own page and its own results.

Does Shillong Night play on Sundays?

Yes. Shillong Night does not take Sunday off, so Sunday evenings appear in the list like any other day.

What is the Shillong Night Teer common number?

It is the set of Direct, House and Ending numbers TeerOnline publishes for Shillong Night Teer alongside the FR and SR result, shown on the homepage (see the link above). It is a record of published numbers, not a forecast.
