Result guide

Shillong, Khanapara and Juwai Teer results in one place

Today’s Teer results for Shillong, Khanapara and Juwai are on this page, one section per game. TeerOnline covers eight games (Shillong, Juwai and Khanapara Morning, the Juwai, Shillong and Khanapara day games, Shillong Night and Shillong Night 2), each with its First Round (FR) and Second Round (SR), its own schedule and its Common Numbers. All times are IST.

Use the game navigator to jump to a game, or open its previous results page to check any earlier date.

What is Teer?

Teer is a traditional archery game rooted in Khasi culture and played in Meghalaya. Archers shoot at a cylindrical bamboo target roughly 50 yards away over two rounds, First Round (FR) and Second Round (SR); the number of archers and arrows in each round is set by the organiser. The declared result is the last two digits of the total number of arrows that hit the target in each round.

Teer is regulated in Meghalaya under the Meghalaya Amusements and Betting Tax (Amendment) Act, 1982. Shillong, Khanapara and Juwai Teer are normally off on Sundays; the Morning and Night games run every day.

TeerOnline does not organise any game, does not predict results, and does not guarantee any number.

Which game is which?

Shillong Teer, Khanapara Teer and Juwai Teer are the three day games, and they are normally off on Sundays. Each town also has a Morning game earlier in the day. Shillong Night runs in the evening, and Shillong Night 2 starts at 11:10 PM with its SR at 12:10 AM, which belongs to the same playing date. Juwai is also spelled Jowai.

FR & SR: two rounds, one session

FR means First Round and SR means Second Round. They belong to the same selected game and should not be mixed with another Morning, Day or Night session.

Understanding Common Numbers

Common Numbers are shown inside the same game section as the FR and SR result. Each set lists Direct numbers together with House and Ending digits. When a current set is unavailable, the page simply says it has not been published yet instead of showing an older set as current.

Previous Results by game

Each game has its own Previous Results page. Use it to check completed FR and SR records by date without mixing Morning, Day and Night sessions.

Glossary

Common Number
The Direct, House and Ending numbers TeerOnline publishes for a game after its result. A record of published numbers, not a forecast.
House
The first digit of a two-digit result. For a result of 47, the House is 4.
Ending
The second digit of a two-digit result. For a result of 47, the Ending is 7.
Dream Number
A traditional reference linking a dream subject to a number, unrelated to how any game's result is actually declared. See Dream Numbers.
Formula
A way of combining past House and Ending digits to narrow a list of numbers. A Teer Formula does not predict or guarantee a result.

No number, dream reference or formula can predict a Teer result. See the disclaimer.

Help

Teer Results FAQ

What time are today’s Teer results announced?

Each game follows its own FR and SR schedule shown above in IST.

What do FR and SR mean?

FR is First Round and SR is Second Round.

Which games are off on Sunday?

Juwai Teer, Shillong Teer and Khanapara Teer are normally off on Sundays.

Why is Shillong Night 2 SR after midnight?

The 12:10 AM SR completes the Night 2 session that began with the 11:10 PM FR.

When are Common Numbers published?

The usual publication time is shown inside each game section.

Where can I check Previous Results?

Use the game-specific Previous Results button inside the game you are viewing.

What are House and Ending?

House is the first digit and Ending is the second digit of a two-digit result.

What is the difference between Shillong Teer, Shillong Morning and Shillong Night?

They are separate games with their own times and results. Shillong Morning is the first game of the day (FR 10:30 AM, SR 11:30 AM), Shillong Teer is the afternoon game (FR 4:15 PM, SR 5:15 PM) and Shillong Night is the evening game (FR 8:45 PM, SR 9:45 PM). Shillong Night 2 follows later at night.

Is Juwai the same as Jowai?

Yes, both spellings mean the same town. TeerOnline uses Juwai and lists each Juwai game once.
