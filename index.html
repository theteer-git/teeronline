Today’s Shillong Teer Result: What to Check

The live result card is built around four details that should be checked together: the displayed date, the FR number, the SR number and the current status. This matters because Shillong Teer has two rounds and the numbers may not appear at the same time. A published FR does not imply that SR is also available.

1. Confirm the date

Make sure the date above the result belongs to the day you intend to check. This is the simplest way to avoid confusing a recent record with today’s result.

2. Read FR and SR separately

FR is the First Round and SR is the Second Round. Each has its own scheduled time, number and publication state.

3. Check the status

Pending means the round is not yet recorded. A completed number is displayed only for the relevant date and round.

4. Use history for verification

The recent-results panel and previous-results page are for checking older records. They do not replace the live card.

How TeerOnline Presents Result Information

TeerOnline publishes result information and historical reference material. It does not organise the game, sell tickets, accept bets or create results. The page is designed to avoid several common errors: carrying an older number into a new date, treating a pending round as zero, combining FR and SR into one value, or presenting a historical statistic as a live result.

When a concern is reported, the useful details are the game name, exact date, affected round and disputed number. A confirmed correction should be limited to that record so unrelated dates remain unchanged. The contact page is available for such reports.

Result publication may be delayed by source availability, connectivity or verification. A delay does not change the declared schedule, and refreshing the page cannot generate or alter a result; it only requests the latest available data.

Shillong Teer Result Questions

What time is the Shillong Teer result declared?

The regular Shillong Teer First Round is scheduled for 4:15 PM IST and the Second Round for 5:15 PM IST on playing days. Online publication can appear slightly later while the result is recorded and checked.

What do FR and SR mean?

FR means First Round and SR means Second Round. Each round has its own two-digit result and publication status.

What does pending mean on the result card?

Pending means a valid result has not yet been recorded for that date and round. It is not zero, and an older result is not carried forward.

Why can FR appear while SR is still pending?

The two rounds are published independently. FR can therefore be complete while SR remains pending until the second-round result becomes available.

Is Shillong Teer normally held on Sunday?

The regular Shillong Teer day game is generally not scheduled on Sunday. The date and status displayed on the page should be checked for the current day.

Where can I check older Shillong Teer results?

Use the Shillong Teer Previous Results link beside the live card to review available date-wise FR and SR records.

Do common numbers predict the next result?

No. Common numbers and statistics describe selected historical records only. They do not determine or guarantee a future FR or SR.

How can I report a result concern?

Use the contact page and include the game name, date, round and the number you believe needs review. A confirmed correction should affect only the relevant record.
