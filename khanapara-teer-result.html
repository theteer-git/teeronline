About the Khanapara Teer result

Khanapara Teer is the afternoon game played at Khanapara, on the edge of Guwahati and across the Meghalaya border. Its First Round is declared at 4:25 PM IST and its Second Round at 5:00 PM IST, only thirty-five minutes later. The game is off on Sundays.

Khanapara Morning is a separate game with its own page. The Khanapara afternoon rounds are not the same as the Shillong afternoon rounds, even though they fall at similar times.

Each result is a two-digit number from 00 to 99 and keeps its leading zero, so 04 is not the same as 4. To see how the rounds are declared, read how Teer results are declared; for a plain explanation of House and Ending, see how to read previous results.

Khanapara Teer result: common questions

When is the Khanapara Teer result declared?

The First Round (FR) is declared at 4:25 PM IST and the Second Round (SR) at 5:00 PM IST. It is off on Sundays.

Why does a Khanapara Teer round show dashes?

A dash means that round has not been published yet. A result appears here only after it has been checked, and the published time is shown beside it.

Where can I see earlier Khanapara Teer results?

Every earlier Khanapara Teer result on record is on the previous results page, grouped by month and searchable by date, number or round.

Are the Common Numbers on this page a prediction?

No. Common Numbers are shown for reference next to the declared results. They are not a forecast and TeerOnline does not predict results.
