Juwai Teer previous results and the Jowai spelling

This page lists results for Juwai Teer, the afternoon Juwai game. Some visitors search for the same game as “Jowai Teer”; it is one series, listed once under the Juwai name. Juwai Morning is a different game and has its own page.

Juwai Teer normally runs Monday to Saturday, with FR at 2:30 PM IST and SR at 3:15 PM IST, earlier in the afternoon than the Shillong Teer and Khanapara Teer games. It is off on Sunday. Check the date and round in each row rather than comparing with another game’s timing.

Juwai Teer at a glance

  • Game: Juwai Teer, the afternoon Juwai game, also spelled Jowai
  • First Round (FR): 2:30 PM IST
  • Second Round (SR): 3:15 PM IST
  • Playing days: Monday to Saturday; Sunday is off
  • Filters: date, number, weekday and round

House and Ending in Juwai Teer results

A result such as 63 has House 6 and Ending 3, and FR and SR each have their own House and Ending. Results below ten keep a leading zero (05, not 5). For how these digits are used in Teer formulas, see the Teer Formula guide.

Juwai Morning and the other day games

The morning game has its own page: Juwai Morning previous results. The other day games are Shillong Teer and Khanapara Teer.

Juwai Teer results: common questions

Is Juwai Teer the same as Jowai Teer?

Yes. Both spellings refer to the same game and the same results. This page uses the Juwai spelling, and every result is listed once.

Why does Juwai Teer come earlier than the other day games?

Juwai Teer runs FR at 2:30 PM and SR at 3:15 PM IST, earlier in the afternoon than Shillong Teer (4:15 PM and 5:15 PM) and Khanapara Teer (4:25 PM and 5:00 PM).

Where can I find Juwai Morning results?

Juwai Morning is a separate game with FR at 10:30 AM and SR at 11:30 AM IST. Its results are on the Juwai Morning previous results page, linked above.
