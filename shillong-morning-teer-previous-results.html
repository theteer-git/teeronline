How to Interpret Shillong Morning Teer Historical Records

First Round (FR) and Second Round (SR)

FR and SR are independent historical outcomes recorded for the same playing date. When researching older results, compare each round separately instead of assuming a relationship between them.

Leading Zeroes

results values are always stored in two-digit format. Numbers such as 01, 07 and 09 should never be shortened because they represent different records from 10, 70 and 90.

Historical Frequency

Some numbers appear more often than others over a selected period. Frequency summaries describe previous records only and should not be interpreted as forecasts.

House and Ending Digits

Researchers sometimes compare the first digit (house digit) and second digit (ending digit) across historical results. These observations are descriptive rather than predictive.

Repeated Numbers and Pairs

Repeated values or FR/SR pairs may be found across different dates. Their appearance simply reflects historical occurrence and does not indicate that they will repeat again.

Weekday Comparisons

Grouping results by weekday can help organize historical analysis. Similar weekday outcomes across different weeks are coincidences within the results and should not be treated as future indicators.

Responsible Interpretation

The purpose of this archive is verification, research and historical reference. Patterns, frequencies, repeated numbers and digit analysis cannot guarantee any future Shillong Morning Teer result.
