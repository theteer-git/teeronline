About Shillong Morning Teer

Shillong Morning is the first game of the Teer day, with FR at 10:30 AM IST and SR at 11:30 AM IST. Unlike Shillong Teer, it does not take Sunday off, so a week in this list can contain seven playing days.

It is a separate series from Shillong Teer, Shillong Night and Shillong Night 2. Each has its own page and its own results, and none of them are mixed into this table.

The Day of Week filter includes Sunday for this game. Choose Sunday to list only the Sunday mornings, something that is not possible for Shillong Teer, which does not run on Sundays.

Shillong Morning Teer is played in Shillong, the same city as Shillong Teer. First Round (FR) is declared at 10:30 AM and Second Round (SR) at 11:30 AM IST.

Shillong Morning Teer at a glance

  • Game: Shillong Morning, the first game of the day
  • First Round (FR): 10:30 AM IST
  • Second Round (SR): 11:30 AM IST
  • Playing days: every day of the week, with no Sunday off
  • Filters: date, number, weekday and round

Looking up a Shillong Morning result

Select Filter to open the search panel. Find a number lists every morning on which a two-digit result (00 to 99) was declared, with weekday and round as optional narrowing; Same date, every year checks one calendar date across every year on record; Last 7 <weekday>s repeats today's day of the week. Newest results come first, and Reset clears the filter.

House and Ending in Shillong Morning results

The first digit of a result is its House and the second is its Ending, so 26 has House 2 and Ending 6. FR and SR are read separately, and a leading zero is kept (07 stays 07). The Teer Formula guide has worked examples of both.

Shillong Morning Teer results: common questions

Is Shillong Morning played on Sundays?

Yes. Unlike Shillong Teer, Shillong Morning does not take Sunday off, so Sunday mornings appear in the list like any other day.

Is Shillong Morning the same as Shillong Teer?

No. They are separate games with different times and separate results: Shillong Morning runs at 10:30 AM and 11:30 AM IST, while Shillong Teer runs at 4:15 PM and 5:15 PM IST.

How do I check one particular morning?

Select Filter, choose Same date, every year, pick the date and select Show results. The FR and SR for that morning appear together, and Reset returns the table to the newest results.

What is the Shillong Morning Teer common number?

It is the set of Direct, House and Ending numbers TeerOnline publishes for Shillong Morning Teer alongside the FR and SR result, shown on the homepage (see the link above). It is a record of published numbers, not a forecast.
