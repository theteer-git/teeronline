Understanding the Khanapara Morning Teer Archive

FR, SR and playing date

FR means First Round and SR means Second Round. Read both values with the date shown in the same row. For late-night games, the publication clock may cross midnight while the result still belongs to the original playing date.

Searching by number

Enter a complete two-digit value. Leading zeroes matter: 04 is not the same result as 40. A match shows where that value appeared historically; it does not indicate what will appear next.

Corrections and record quality

When a historical entry is verified as incorrect, only the affected date and round should be corrected. Other rows remain unchanged. This keeps the archive useful for checking older results while avoiding unnecessary rewrites of unrelated records.

Historical data is not a prediction

Repeated values, weekday frequency and earlier pairs describe the past only. They cannot guarantee a future FR or SR. TeerOnline provides records for information and comparison and does not sell tickets or accept bets.
