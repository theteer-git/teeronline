How the Page Handles Updates and Corrections

The page checks the public result feed more frequently around the declared result windows and less frequently outside them. Publication time can vary slightly from the declared schedule. The displayed status is therefore more useful than repeatedly refreshing before a round has been recorded.

Understanding Common Numbers and Statistics
