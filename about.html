📖 Our Story

TeerOnline.com was founded with a simple mission: to provide accurate, timely, and reliable Teer results for enthusiasts across Meghalaya. What started as a small personal project has grown into a comprehensive resource for Shillong, Khanapara, Juwai, and Night Teer results.

We understand the importance of having access to authentic Teer data. Our platform is built on the principles of transparency, accuracy, and informational integrity. We do not promote gambling or betting; instead, we serve as a historical results and reference point for those interested in this traditional Meghalaya archery game.

Our publishing standard

TeerOnline separates live result publication from historical analysis. Result pages show the current FR and SR status, Previous Results pages preserve dated records, and reference guides explain terminology without presenting predictions as facts.

When a result is pending, delayed or only partly available, the page should continue to show that state rather than substitute an earlier number. Corrections are applied when stronger information becomes available, and the latest visible value should remain consistent with the historical record.
